# Boxer Property – New Building Onboarding Process Optimization

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository documents a **business consulting engagement** conducted with **Boxer Property**, where our team worked directly with **Mark Vecchio, COO**, to analyze and redesign the onboarding process for newly acquired properties under third-party management.

As Boxer Property scaled its third-party management portfolio, onboarding new buildings became increasingly **manual, time-consuming, and error-prone**, creating operational bottlenecks and limiting scalability. Our role was to act as **external consultants**, diagnose the problem end-to-end, and propose a **future-state process** supported by automation and standardized workflows.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Onboarding a single new building required:

* **800+ person-hours** of manual effort
* **Over one month** per building
* Sequential processing (one building at a time)
* Repeated follow-ups for missing or disorganized documents

Key challenges included:

* Highly manual document extraction and data entry
* Disorganized incoming files (digital, scanned, missing)
* Limited visibility into onboarding status
* High risk of errors and missed compliance items
* Poor scalability as third-party managed properties increased

---

## 🧠 Consulting Approach (What We Did)

### 1. Client Discovery & Problem Framing

* Conducted discovery meetings with the COO to understand Boxer Property’s business model, growth strategy, and operational constraints
* Mapped the **current-state onboarding workflow** across departments
* Quantified effort, timelines, and capacity constraints

### 2. Current-State Process Analysis

* Identified key bottlenecks caused by manual document handling
* Analyzed exception loops created by missing or misclassified documents
* Assessed risks related to data quality, safety, and compliance

### 3. Future-State Process Design

* Designed a **standardized onboarding journey** with a single document intake point
* Proposed intelligent document classification, extraction, and validation
* Introduced **human-in-the-loop (HITL)** controls for low-confidence cases
* Built exception handling for missing documents through automated case creation

### 4. Technology Enablement (Conceptual)

* Designed a high-level architecture using:

  * RPA (UiPath Orchestrator & Document Understanding)
  * OCR-based field extraction
  * Integration with M-Files and Brava
* Defined rule-based routing, confidence scoring, and audit logging

### 5. Pilot Planning & Metrics

* Scoped a **30-day pilot** for a single Houston property
* Defined success metrics across speed, accuracy, efficiency, and reliability
* Identified key risks, assumptions, and mitigation strategies

---

## 🔄 Current vs. Proposed Process

### Current State

* Manual document sorting and data entry
* Department-by-department sign-offs
* Continuous exception loops
* No clear completion point

### Proposed State

* Single intake folder for all onboarding documents
* Automated document classification and tagging
* Structured comparison of required vs. received documents
* Dashboard-driven visibility and final sign-off
* Parallel onboarding of multiple properties

---

## 🚀 Expected Impact

The proposed solution delivers:

* **40%+ reduction** in onboarding time
* Significant reduction in manual labor
* Improved data accuracy and compliance
* Real-time visibility into onboarding status
* Scalable onboarding aligned with Boxer’s growth strategy

Strategically, the solution positions onboarding as a **competitive advantage** rather than an operational constraint.

## ⚠️ Key Assumptions & Risks

**Assumptions**:

* Access to prior manager property data
* Documents are digitizable and legible
* Core platforms support integration
* Standardization across properties is achievable

**Risks & Mitigation**:

* Variable document quality → Human-in-the-loop review
* Misclassification → Confidence scoring & validation
* Missing data → Automated case creation
* Adoption challenges → Phased rollout & training
